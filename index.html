About us

We are Romina and Vicente, bilingual licensed nurses. Our background in nursing has trained us to advocate for patients, translate medical jargon, and coordinate care. Having worked within both the Spanish and U.S. healthcare systems, we know how confusing healthcare can feel when language, culture and unfamiliar processes get in the way. Our goal is to make your journey easier and less stressful. We provide advocacy and support.

Common questions

Do you provide medical care?

No. We provide advocacy, interpretation and health-system guidance. We don't diagnose or treat, and we don't replace your doctors.

Who are you?

We are Romina and Vicente, bilingual licensed nurses who have worked in both the Spanish and U.S. healthcare systems.

Can you come to any hospital or clinic in Valencia?

We cover Valencia city and nearby areas, public and private. A travel supplement may apply beyond 50 km or 45 minutes, so ask us about locations further out.

Do I need private insurance?

No. We help with both the public system (SNS) and private care, and can explain which fits your situation.

How do I register for a health card (tarjeta sanitaria)?

We can explain the steps, help you prepare what you need, and help you find your assigned clinic. Paperwork and procedures are quoted in advance.

How do I get an appointment with a routine doctor?

We help you understand how to reach your GP, book or change appointments, and prepare your questions. If you prefer, we can contact the clinic for you or come with you.

I'm struggling to get tests or screenings. Can you help?

Yes. We help you understand referrals, coordinate follow-up appointments and keep everything organized, so the tests you need don't get lost between visits.

When should I go to urgent care instead of a regular check-up?

We can walk you through the options and help you decide where to go. In an emergency, call 112 first.

Can you explain my diagnosis or test results?

Yes. We translate reports and results into plain language so you can discuss them with your doctor. We don't diagnose or give medical advice.

Can you translate my medical documents?

Yes, English–Spanish translation of medical documents, from 24 € for up to 2 pages. Sworn translation is available on quote.

Can you keep my family informed?

Yes, always with your permission. A plain-language recap for one family member is available as an add-on, by call or email.

What happens on the free intro call?

Fifteen minutes. You tell us what support you need, we answer your questions and help you plan the next steps. There is no obligation.

How much does it cost?

Prices start at 16 € for simple clinic inquiries, and appointment support is 39 € per hour (2 h minimum, travel included). Packages and a monthly membership are also available. See the Pricing section for everything, VAT included.

What is the difference between a package and the Companion membership?

Packages cover a defined piece of work: Coordination (up to 5 hours, 195 €) or Comprehensive (up to 10 hours, 385 €). Sol Health Companion is 120 € a month with a 3-month minimum, and suits ongoing support.

Are evenings, weekends and short-notice bookings possible?

Yes. A 20% surcharge applies to bookings with less than 24 h notice, before 08:00 or after 19:00, and on weekends and public holidays.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

Is my health information private?

Yes. We handle it under GDPR and only with your consent. Please don't send medical details through this website; we'll share them securely after we speak.

Can you help in an emergency?

In an emergency call 112 first. We can then support you by phone or join you as soon as possible.
